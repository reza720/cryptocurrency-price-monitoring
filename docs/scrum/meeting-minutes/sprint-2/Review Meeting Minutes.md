The objective of the meeting was to review the work completed by the development team during Sprint 2, evaluate the project's progress, discuss the implemented features, and confirm whether all planned sprint items had been completed.

## Discussion Summary
At the beginning of the meeting, Atifa Rahimi hosted the session and explained the purpose of the Sprint 2 Review Meeting. The team discussed the development work completed during the sprint and reviewed the overall progress of the Cryptocurrency Price Monitoring System.

The development team reported that all planned items for Sprint 2 had been completed and that the application was now working. The team discussed the implemented features and how the completed components worked together to achieve the project's objectives.

The team discussed the GitHub Actions configuration, which allows the monitoring script to run automatically every 20 minutes. This automation enables the application to check cryptocurrency prices periodically without requiring manual execution.

The team also reviewed the validation mechanisms implemented to ensure that the application receives and processes valid input. In addition, the team discussed the CRUD scripts developed for managing cryptocurrency coins and email addresses, including creating, retrieving, updating, and deleting records.

The discussion covered the integration of the completed components, including database configuration, cryptocurrency price monitoring, email management, input validation, and automated execution. The team confirmed that all planned sprint items had been completed and that the application was functioning as expected.

Atifa Rahimi facilitated the discussion and helped the team review its completed work and confirm the achievement of the Sprint 2 objectives. Ghulam Mahfoz Osmani was absent from the meeting.

## Sprint Review Outcome
The team concluded that all planned items for Sprint 2 had been completed. The Cryptocurrency Price Monitoring System was working, with input validations, CRUD scripts for coins and email addresses, and GitHub Actions configured to run the monitoring script automatically every 20 minutes.
