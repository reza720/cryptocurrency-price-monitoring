# Sprint 2 — Sprint Retrospective Meeting Report

## Meeting Information
- **Meeting Type:** Sprint Retrospective Meeting
- **Sprint:** Sprint 2
- **Duration:** 10 minutes
- **Host:** Reza Panahi

## Participants
- Atifa Rahimi
- Reza Panahi
- Ainullah Amery

## Meeting Objective
The objective of the meeting was to reflect on the team's experience during Sprint 2, identify the challenges encountered during development, discuss what could be improved, and suggest future enhancements for the Cryptocurrency Price Monitoring System.

## Discussion Summary
At the beginning of the meeting, Reza Panahi hosted the session and explained the purpose of the Sprint 2 Retrospective Meeting. The team reflected on the development process, discussed the problems encountered during the sprint, and identified opportunities to improve the system and the team's workflow.

The team discussed database connection problems encountered during development. These issues affected the process of connecting the application to the database and required troubleshooting to ensure that the application could access and manage its data correctly.

Another topic was GitHub Actions failures. The team discussed difficulties encountered while configuring and running the automated workflow. These issues highlighted the importance of checking environment variables, workflow configuration, and execution logs when troubleshooting automation failures.

The team also discussed potential improvements to the system in future development. One proposed enhancement was adding authentication to control access to the application and protect its functionality. The team also considered developing a user interface (UI) to make the system easier to use without relying entirely on scripts or command-line operations.

Additionally, the team discussed implementing logging to improve monitoring and troubleshooting. Appropriate logs would help identify errors, track script execution, and understand failures in database connections and automated workflows.

Reza Panahi facilitated the discussion and encouraged the team to identify lessons learned from Sprint 2. The meeting focused on understanding the challenges encountered and determining how the team could improve the system's reliability, usability, and maintainability in future development.
